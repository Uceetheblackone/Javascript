A Basic Walk Through Solar Installation

A beginner-friendly guide to setting up a residential solar system.

Site Assessment

The first step is evaluating your property’s suitability for solar. This includes checking roof orientation, shading, and structural integrity. A professional assessment helps determine the optimal system size.

System Design

Based on your energy needs and site conditions, an installer designs a solar system layout—typically including panels, an inverter, and optionally a battery. This design must meet local regulations and utility requirementse.

Permits & Approvals

Before installation, you'll need permits from your local authority or utility company. Installers often handle this process, ensuring your system is compliant with codes and eligible for incentives.

Installation

The physical installation usually takes 1–3 days. Panels are mounted, wiring is done, and the inverter is connected. After that, the system is inspected and connected to the grid or battery storage.

Activation & Monitoring

Once inspected and approved, your system goes live! Most systems include monitoring software so you can track your energy production and usage in real time.
